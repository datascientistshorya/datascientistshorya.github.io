Working method

Analytics should make the next action clearer.

I start with the outcome a stakeholder needs: improve a funnel, understand revenue, identify a risk pattern, or prioritize a customer group. Then I identify the data, the metric, and the analytical method that can credibly support that decision.

A

Question

Define the decision, audience, scope, and success measure.

B

Evidence

Clean and investigate data while documenting assumptions.

C

Action

Present findings as a practical recommendation or dashboard.
